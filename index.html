Ghostlight

An atmospheric texture instrument. Hold a chord and see what answers.

In testing · download coming soon

Ghostlight has five characters and a row of knobs with names like Tension, Erosion, Breath and Anger. What they do, and how they change from one character to the next, is left for you to find out.

It was built for the stage: every control can be mapped to MIDI, and it stays light enough to keep a whole concert of patches loaded.

  • FormatsAU · VST3 · Standalone
  • SystemmacOS 11+, Apple silicon & Intel
  • HostsLogic Pro, MainStage, any VST3 host
  • CPUUnder 5% of one core per instance

About

iteration1 is a one-person studio making the instruments its founder wants to play: things that feel alive under your hands, reward exploring, and hold up in a live set.

Ghostlight is released free, with its source code, so you can see how it works or build your own.
